What to look for

The input is c + A cos(k₁u + φₐ) + B cos(k₂v + φ_b) on the N×N torus, so its DFT has exactly five entries: DC and the four axis points ±(k₁,0), ±(0,k₂). Any affine f keeps it that way. Any other f creates new entries. The ones that matter for modular addition are the sum term at ±(k₁,k₂), a wave in k₁u+k₂v, and its unavoidable twin the difference term at ±(k₁,−k₂): replacing v by −v leaves the input unchanged, so for every f these two have equal magnitude. Shifting u and v by π negates the input but fixes cos(k₁u+k₂v), so only the even part of f about the bias produces them; the odd part only feeds the axes and odd harmonics. The phase of the sum term is always φₐ+φ_b and that of the difference term φₐ−φ_b. In complex-coefficient terms the sum term is the product a·b of the two inputs (phases add), which is what modular addition needs. Try ReLU at c=0 (coefficient 8A/3π² ≈ 0.27A when A=B, and the same for |t|/2), then slide the bias to see it vanish once f is affine on the whole range, then compare tanh, GELU, and t² while raising A.
